Floorplanning is the initial phase of physical implementation where die size, core boundary, I/O pad placement, macro placement, and power grid network (PGN) architecture are defined. The goal is to minimize total die area, prevent routing congestion, reduce total wire length, and optimize timing paths before standard cell placement. Proper macro placement with adequate halo spacing prevents macro blockage and thermal hotspots.
